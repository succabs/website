## Why I Started Running Again After 10 Years

Running used to be a regular part of my life. In high school, a 5K in 25 minutes was normal for me, and during military service I even broke the 3000m mark in the Cooper test. After that, life drifted in other directions: studying, late nights, long sessions at the computer, and later on, full-time desk work.

Now at 29, I wanted to get back into shape. The surprising thing that finally pushed me into action was buying the **Suunto Run sports watch**. This post is both a personal comeback story and a Suunto Run review from the perspective of someone returning to running after a long break.

---

## Why I Picked the Suunto Run

Before buying anything, I compared different running watches. Most of the competition in this price range seemed outdated, especially on the software side. I wanted something new enough to receive proper updates.

Suunto caught my eye for two reasons:

- It’s a **Finnish brand**, so I’m familiar with them
- The **Suunto Run** had only recently been released

Fresh hardware usually means a longer update cycle. At around **€250**, the price felt reasonable for a beginner-focused running watch.

The main compromise: this model uses a new operating system, meaning **no Suunto Plus Store**. So apps and integrations like Strava aren’t available. For me, that wasn’t a dealbreaker. I didn’t buy the watch for leaderboards or social features — I bought it to help myself get moving again.

---

## Suunto Run Review: One Month of Real Use

After a month of wearing it every day, here’s what stood out.

### Activity Tracking Works Exactly As It Should

Running, walking, and general movement tracking have been reliable. I don’t need advanced metrics — just accurate basics — and the watch handles that with no issues.

### Daily Metrics Are Simple but Motivating

Step goals, heart rate, and sleep tracking give a nice daily overview. Seeing progress visualized makes it easier to stay consistent.

### Notifications Need Some Work

On iOS, notifications don’t clear automatically when you dismiss them on your phone, so you have to clear them manually on the watch. It’s not a huge issue, but it's noticeable.

### Battery Life Is Great

Even with multiple GPS runs per week, the battery lasts several days.

### Design Is Clean and Lightweight

I bought the black model. It looks good with everything and doesn’t feel bulky or overly “sporty.”

As someone whose last wearable was a cheap Honor Band 5 years ago, this felt like a big upgrade.

---

## The Software Situation

The Suunto app is clearly improving. An AI coach was released recently, though it doesn’t fully integrate with the Suunto Run yet — for example, the watch doesn’t guide you through daily workouts during a run.

But Suunto keeps adding widgets and features natively, which partly compensates for the lack of third-party integrations.

The question that remains is whether Suunto will continue evolving this OS in the long term or if the Run ends up being a one-off experiment. Time will tell.

---

## How the Suunto Run Motivated Me to Move More

The biggest surprise is how much the watch influenced my habits.

- I’m running multiple times a week again
- I walk more to hit step goals
- I pay attention to sleep because I want to see a decent score
- My “fitness age” dropped from 38 to 35 in a month

The watch isn’t magic, but it keeps the idea of movement in my mind throughout the day. That alone has been worth the price.

---

## Is the Suunto Run Worth It?

Here’s the bottom line of this Suunto Run review:

If you’re an advanced runner who wants deep integrations, detailed analytics, and Strava syncing, this isn’t the watch for you.

But if you’re a **beginner, casual runner, or someone returning to running** after a long break, it’s an excellent match.

### Pros

- Reliable tracking
- Good battery life
- Comfortable and simple
- Fair price (~€250)
- Motivating for beginners

### Cons

- Limited integrations
- Notifications feel unfinished on iOS

For me, these trade-offs were easy to accept. The watch helped me start running again after ten years, and that alone makes it a success.
