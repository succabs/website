# Refined poster

Keep the existing text, project categories and page layouts. Personality comes from Arttu's voice, photography and work.

- Charcoal #1C2126 is the canvas; muted slate #262E35 distinguishes panels.
- Warm off-white #F3F0E6 anchors titles; a deeper charcoal anchors the footer.
- Mustard yellow #E5C763 is reserved for links, small rules and interaction states.
- Body text is #E3E3DD; secondary text is #B7BEC2.
- Anton is reserved for page titles and the wordmark. Inter handles navigation, body and smaller headings.
- Use thin borders, square corners, generous spacing and natural photographs.
- Avoid comic stamps, tilted elements, heavy offset shadows, textures and entrance animations.
- Preserve visible focus indicators, usable mobile navigation and reduced-motion support.
